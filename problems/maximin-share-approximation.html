Short Statement

Background & Context

Maximin share is the value an agent can ensure by partitioning goods into n bundles and receiving her least-valued bundle. Exact simultaneous MMS can fail for additive goods, motivating universal approximate guarantees. Algorithmic guarantees and impossibility instances must be stated separately for a fixed n and uniformly over all n.
