Short Statement

Determine the optimal randomized competitive ratio for Adwords when advertiser budgets are initially unknown.

Open Problem

Determine the optimal small-bid randomized ratio within the reviewed [0.522,1−1/e][0.522,1-1/e][0.522,1−1/e] bounds; framework-specific barriers do not settle this target.

Background & Context

Unknown budgets remove the budget fractions used by classical Adwords allocation rules. The feedback and truncation convention are part of the model, as is the small-bids limit; guarantees for known budgets or arbitrary bid sizes do not transfer automatically.
