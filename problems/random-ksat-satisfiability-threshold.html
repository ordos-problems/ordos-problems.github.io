Short Statement

Determine the exact satisfiability threshold for random kkk-SAT at small fixed kkk, especially k=3k=3k=3.

Open Problem

Prove the exact random kkk-SAT satisfiability threshold for fixed small kkk, in particular k=3k=3k=3. The open part is not sharpness of the transition in a qualitative sense, but the rigorous identification of the threshold value for these small kkk.

Background & Context

Random kkk-SAT is a central model at the boundary of combinatorics, probability, statistical physics, and computational complexity. A formula on nnn Boolean variables is formed by drawing m=⌊αn⌋m=\lfloor \alpha n\rfloorm=⌊αn⌋ random clauses, each containing kkk literals. For small clause density α\alphaα, the formula is satisfiable with high probability; for large α\alphaα, it is unsatisfiable with high probability. Physics predicts a detailed sequence of phase transitions, and the satisfiability threshold is the most basic one. For k=2k=2k=2, the exact threshold is 111. For every fixed k≥3k\ge3k≥3, Friedgut proved sharp-threshold behavior, but this theorem does not identify a clean limiting threshold value. Ding, Sly, and Sun proved the predicted threshold for all sufficiently large kkk, a major breakthrough. The historically famous remaining case is small fixed kkk, especially random 3-SAT, where rigorous upper and lower bounds are close numerically but the exact limiting threshold is still unknown.

What Is Known

The k=2k=2k=2 threshold is exactly 111. Friedgut proved that random kkk-SAT has a sharp threshold for each fixed kkk, but the theorem allows the threshold location to vary with nnn and does not identify the limiting constant. Achlioptas, Moore, Coja-Oghlan, Panagiotou, and others developed increasingly sharp rigorous upper and lower bounds and established the correct asymptotic order in kkk. Ding, Sly, and Sun proved the satisfiability conjecture for all sufficiently large kkk, matching the one-step replica-symmetry-breaking prediction. For small kkk, especially k=3k=3k=3, the best rigorous numerical bounds are close to the predicted value, but the exact threshold and the full finite-kkk condensation/satisfiability analysis are still not proved.

Remaining open problem. Prove the exact random kkk-SAT satisfiability threshold for fixed small kkk, in particular k=3k=3k=3. The open part is not sharpness of the transition in a qualitative sense, but the rigorous identification of the threshold value for these small kkk.

Progress & Literature

Latest progress (Partial progress). The exact threshold is known for k=2k=2k=2 and for all sufficiently large kkk. The small fixed cases such as 3-SAT remain open despite extensive rigorous and physics-based work. The qualitative transition picture is well established: below the threshold solutions exist with high probability and above it they do not. Friedgut's theorem supplies sharpness but not a usable formula for the threshold. The large-kkk breakthrough of Ding, Sly, and Sun validates the physics prediction in a regime where the one-step replica-symmetry-breaking calculations can be made rigorous. Small kkk is harder because error terms, local dependencies, and condensation effects cannot be hidden in asymptotic large-kkk expansions. The concrete gap is to convert the physics prediction for k=3k=3k=3 and other small values into a rigorous threshold theorem.
