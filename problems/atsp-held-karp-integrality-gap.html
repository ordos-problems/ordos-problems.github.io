Short Statement

The asymmetric metric TSP Held-Karp integrality gap lies between 222 and 171717; its exact value remains open.

Open Problem

Determine the exact integrality gap of the Held-Karp LP for asymmetric metric TSP, closing the gap between the lower bound 222 and the best known upper bound 171717.

Background & Context

Asymmetric TSP replaces undirected metrics by directed metrics, and this destroys the parity structure that makes Christofides' algorithm work. The standard Held-Karp LP is still the central relaxation: it enforces one unit of in-flow and out-flow at each vertex plus subtour-elimination constraints. For many years it was not even known whether this relaxation had a constant integrality gap. Svensson, Tarnawski, and Vegh proved the breakthrough constant-factor approximation, analyzed against the LP, thereby establishing a bounded gap. Traub and Vygen improved the bound first to 222222 and then to 171717 in their 2024 book. The lower bound remains only 222. The exact integrality gap is therefore a major open quantitative question in directed network design.

What Is Known

Charikar, Goemans, and Karloff constructed ATSP instances with Held-Karp integrality gap at least 222, establishing the main lower-bound benchmark. Before 2018, general ATSP had only logarithmic approximation guarantees, and it was unknown whether the Held-Karp relaxation had any constant gap. Svensson, Tarnawski, and Vegh gave the first constant-factor approximation for general ATSP, implying a constant Held-Karp gap; their analysis yields an integrality-ratio upper bound of 319319319 and an approximation factor 506506506. Progress & Literature

The modern ATSP algorithms reduce global directed connectivity requirements to more local covering and augmentation problems, such as Subtour Partition Cover and structured vertebrate-pair instances. These reductions finally proved that the Held-Karp relaxation is constant-factor roundable, but the constants are far from the lower bound. The gap may reflect inefficiency in the decomposition/augmentation framework, or it may indicate missing lower-bound gadgets with much larger ratio than 222. Progress likely requires either a sharper rounding of thin directed structures or a new family of directed metrics that exposes limitations of Held-Karp.
