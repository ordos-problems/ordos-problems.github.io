Short Statement

Develop sharp cover-time asymptotics for sequentially arriving randomly centered growing balls on compact metric spaces.

Open Problem

Background & Context

Random coverage connects geometric probability, coupon collection, and stochastic geometry. Aldous's growing-ball model uses Poisson arrivals and constant radial growth on a compact metric space. The deterministic integer-time arrivals and general growth function in this record define a broader, different model; the cited concentration theorem does not automatically transfer. Sharp laws now exist for important Poisson Euclidean models, so the research target must distinguish arrival schedules, growth rules, and geometric classes.
