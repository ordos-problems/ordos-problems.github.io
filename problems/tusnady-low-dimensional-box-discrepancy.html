Short Statement

Close the logarithmic gaps in Tusnady's discrepancy problem for axis-parallel boxes in dimensions 2, 3, and 4.

Open Problem

Determine the asymptotic discrepancy of axis-parallel boxes for fixed low dimensions d=2,3,4d=2,3,4d=2,3,4.

Background & Context

Geometric discrepancy asks how evenly finite point sets can be two-colored against geometric ranges. It is a bridge between combinatorics, approximation algorithms, numerical integration, data structures, and randomized rounding. Axis-parallel boxes are the most basic multidimensional range family beyond intervals, and Tusnady's problem is the flagship open question for this setting. The problem is clean: the input is an arbitrary finite point set, the allowed ranges are fixed, and the goal is a worst-case logarithmic exponent. It complements the repository's existing Spencer/Komlos/Beck-Fiala discrepancy entries by focusing on a geometric set system where the best known bounds are dimension-sensitive and where existential and algorithmic bounds must be distinguished.

What Is Known
