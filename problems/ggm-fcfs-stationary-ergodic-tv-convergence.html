Short Statement

For a stable FCFS G/G/mG/G/mG/G/m queue driven only by stationary ergodic interarrival and service sequences, does the Kiefer-Wolfowitz workload vector started empty converge in total variation to the minimal stationary distribution?

Open Problem

Prove or refute total-variation convergence to the minimal stationary distribution for the stable FCFS G/G/mG/G/mG/G/m queue under stationary ergodic input alone.

Background & Context

Single-server queues have an unusually clean stability theory. For a stable G/G/1G/G/1G/G/1 FCFS queue with stationary ergodic input, Loynes' construction gives a unique stationary waiting-time distribution and convergence in total variation from every deterministic initial delay. The natural multi-server analogue is much harder because the state is the ordered vector of residual workloads, not a scalar Lindley recursion. Kiefer and Wolfowitz introduced the workload-vector recursion for the G/G/mG/G/mG/G/m queue, and the empty-start recursion is monotone enough to yield weak convergence to the minimal stationary distribution under stability. What is missing is the stronger coupling/total-variation conclusion under only stationary ergodicity. Extra assumptions such as iid, regenerative, or Markovian input allow Harris or renovation arguments, but those assumptions change the theorem. Foss singled this out as a long-standing open problem because it asks whether the classical one-server robustness extends to the most basic many-server FCFS model without any hidden mixing hypothesis.

What Is Known

For m=1m=1m=1, Loynes' scheme proves existence, uniqueness, and total-variation convergence for the stationary ergodic G/G/1G/G/1G/G/1 queue under the negative-drift stability condition. For m≥2m\ge2m≥2, the Kiefer-Wolfowitz recursion gives a monotone empty-start construction and weak convergence to the minimal stationary distribution, but not total-variation convergence. Foss notes that multi-server queues can have multiple stationary regimes in general, while iid, regenerative, or Markovian assumptions restore uniqueness and total-variation convergence through Harris recurrence or renovation techniques. The precise stationary-ergodic theorem remains open.

Progress & Literature

Latest progress (Partial progress). Weak convergence from the empty state and total-variation convergence under stronger mixing assumptions are known. The unresolved point is whether stationary ergodicity plus stability is enough. Foss, Some Open Problems Related to Stability General G/G/k queue background

The one-server proof works because the workload is the running maximum of a negative-drift stationary ergodic random walk; finite coupling follows from the backward maximum being attained at a finite time. In the multi-server case, the Kiefer-Wolfowitz map preserves enough order to define a minimal stationary workload vector from the empty start, but coordinate sorting and parallel service destroy the scalar random-walk representation. Additional stochastic regularity gives regeneration or small sets, so the issue is not stability itself but whether ergodicity alone forces enough forgetting of initial conditions. The remaining gap is a sharp ergodic theorem for the workload-vector recursion, or an explicit stable stationary ergodic input where weak convergence does not strengthen to total variation.
