Short Statement

The equilateral triangle gives the apparent worst-case gap between the Euclidean minimum spanning tree and the Euclidean Steiner minimum tree, but no complete proof of this sharp constant is known.

Open Problem

Prove or disprove that every finite planar point set satisfies SMT⁡(P)≥(3/2)MST⁡(P)\operatorname{SMT}(P)\ge (\sqrt{3}/2)\operatorname{MST}(P)SMT(P)≥(3​/2)MST(P).

Background & Context

The Euclidean Steiner tree problem is one of the classical network-design problems in geometric optimization. The input is already completely specified by finitely many points; the extra modeling choice is not the issue. What is hard is understanding the sharp loss incurred by the minimum spanning tree heuristic when the true optimum is allowed to introduce Steiner points. This sharp constant matters because MST-based networks are simple, canonical, and easy to compute, while Euclidean Steiner trees expose the geometric value of adding junctions. Gilbert and Pollak formulated the conjecture in 1968. A proof announced by Du and Hwang around 1990 was widely cited for years, but later clarification work identified a serious gap, so the conjecture remains open. The problem has recently become relevant to AI-agent mathematics because Ke, Huang, Shu, He, Gai, and Wang use LLM-generated geometric lemmas plus executable verification functions to improve certified lower bounds toward the conjectured constant.

What Is Known

The equilateral triangle shows that no bound larger than 3/2\sqrt{3}/23​/2 is possible for SMT⁡(P)/MST⁡(P)\operatorname{SMT}(P)/\operatorname{MST}(P)SMT(P)/MST(P). Classical lower-bound work before the 1990s reached a certified lower bound around 0.8240.8240.824 in this convention. Du and Hwang published a claimed proof of the exact 3/2\sqrt{3}/23​/2 bound, but later papers by Innami, Kim, Mashiko, Shiohama and by Ivanov and Tuzhilin explained that the proof has an unresolved gap, so it cannot be used as a resolution. The best widely cited older upper bound on the reciprocal ratio is about 1.21341.21341.2134, while the conjectured reciprocal value is 2/3≈1.15472/\sqrt{3}\approx 1.15472/3​≈1.1547. Thus the open part is not to formulate a new geometric network model; it is to close the remaining constant gap or disprove the sharp equilateral-triangle conjecture.
