Background & Context

Single-sample prophet inequalities replace full distributional knowledge by one historical observation per element. Earlier rank-one and special-matroid guarantees motivated the general question. It is now addressed by the new theorem below.

What Is Known

Abdi, Banihashem, Hajiaghayi, Huang, Mittal, and Singla (arXiv:2611.08869v1), Theorem 1.2 / formal Theorem 3.1, report the optimal 1/21/21/2 guarantee with one independent sample per element for every known matroid and independent nonnegative values. The order must be fixed independently of values, samples, and random coins, but need not be revealed beforehand. The implementation uses O(n2)O(n^2)O(n2) independence queries. The earlier polylogarithmic-sample guarantee is no longer the frontier for this model.
