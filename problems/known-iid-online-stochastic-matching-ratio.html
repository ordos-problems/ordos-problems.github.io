Short Statement

A recent paper reports the optimal asymptotic ratio for unweighted known-i.i.d. stochastic matching; polynomial-time attainment is separate.

Claimed Resolution

Verify the reported asymptotic resolution for the basic unweighted arbitrary-rate model. Exact finite-horizon performance, polynomial-time attainment, and any separate integral-rate optimum are not certified by this audit.

Background & Context

Known-law stochastic matching separates distributional knowledge from foresight of realized arrivals. The relevant benchmark here is expected offline maximum cardinality, not a fluid LP. A new information-theoretic theorem now addresses the basic asymptotic ratio.
