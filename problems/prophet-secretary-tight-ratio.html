Short Statement

Determine the exact optimal competitive ratio for selecting one item from independent non-identical distributions arriving in uniformly random order.

Open Problem

Determine the exact constant within the updated bounds for order-unaware random arrivals.

Background & Context

Prophet secretary combines two classical optimal-stopping models. In prophet inequalities, distributions are known but arrivals may be adversarial; in secretary problems, order is random but values are often adversarial or unknown. Prophet secretary keeps known independent distributions and random arrival order. This is a minimal model for posted-price selling when buyer types are heterogeneous but arrive in random order. The exact constant matters because it quantifies the value of random order over adversarial order. The original result showed that random order beats the 1/21/21/2 prophet-inequality barrier and approaches 1−1/e1-1/e1−1/e with simple nonadaptive thresholds. Later work improved both lower and upper bounds, but the exact worst-case ratio is still not known.

What Is Known

Esfandiari, Hajiaghayi, Liaghat, and Monemizadeh introduced the prophet-secretary model and proved that random order beats the classical adversarial-order 1/21/21/2 barrier, with simple threshold methods reaching the 1−1/e1-1/e1−1/e scale and an early 0.750.750.75 upper bound. Correa, Saona, and Ziliotto developed blind quantile strategies, proved a 0.6650.6650.665 guarantee, showed blind strategies cannot exceed 0.6750.6750.675, and gave a 0.7320.7320.732 upper bound for arbitrary algorithms. Bubna and Chiplunkar improved the random-order hardness to about 0.72540.72540.7254 and separated random order from order selection. Chen, Huang, Li, and Tang gave a 0.6880.6880.688-competitive algorithm in 2024, breaking the blind-strategy barrier. Progress & Literature

Latest progress (Partial progress). Both the algorithmic and impossibility summaries improve; neither paper determines the exact constant. Prophet Secretary Blind strategies Largest-item algorithm Order selection beats random order Mean-only Prophet Inequalities

The model began with simple threshold strategies that already showed a qualitative separation from adversarial-order prophet inequalities. Subsequent work introduced blind strategies, which choose acceptance probabilities as a function of time and value quantile rather than distribution identity, giving cleaner analyses and better constants. The hard examples are delicate because random order symmetrizes the instance, while non-identical distributions still allow rare high values to distort the prophet benchmark. Recent separations among random-order, i.i.d., and order-selection variants show that constants are genuinely model-specific. The remaining task is to identify the extremal distribution family and matching optimal stopping rule. The newer mean-only guarantee transfers to the full-information model, but its restricted-threshold hardness does not bound all known-distribution algorithms.
