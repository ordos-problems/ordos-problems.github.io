Background & Context

Online monotone subsequence selection is a clean sequential-selection problem at the boundary of applied probability, optimal stopping, and online algorithms. A sequence of iid continuous observations arrives one at a time, and the decision maker must irrevocably select an increasing subsequence. The offline longest increasing subsequence has famous 2n2\sqrt n2n​ first-order behavior; the online constrained version has first-order constant 2\sqrt{2}2​. The first-order law is classical. The later second-order analysis resolves the bounded-versus-divergent question formerly posed here.

What Is Known

Samuels and Steele established the first-order scale; Arlotto, Wei, and Xie gave an adaptive policy with logarithmic additive guarantees. Seksenbayev, Acta Applicandae Mathematicae 174, article 6 (2021), Theorem 1, proves the expansion in full_statement directly for fixed nnn.
